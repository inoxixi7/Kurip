## Kurip 0.1.0

First release under the Kurip name (formerly QuickRecorder).

- Works on macOS 26 and later: menu bar controls fixed, menu-bar-only by default
- Redesigned main panel, recording toolbars and option bars
- Audio-only recording from its own popover; quit asks for confirmation
- After recording, a card with Show in Finder, Trim and Share
- Settings in the System Settings style, with search
- Recording menu shows Pause / Resume by state, your hotkeys, and Save Current Frame
- A recording that can't start now explains why instead of crashing
- New languages: Japanese and German; Italian completed; language picker in Settings
- Fixes: frame rate on ProMotion displays, magnifier memory leak, AppleScript app matching
